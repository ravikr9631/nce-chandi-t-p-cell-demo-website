💼 Why Companies Recruit from NCE

Strategic recruitment advantages driving industry leaders to NCE Chandi

Low Cost Hiring

Access high-caliber engineering talent at competitive compensation packages compared to Tier-1 institutes, maximizing recruitment efficiency.

Bulk Hiring

Consolidated talent pool allows companies to recruit across multiple branches (CSE, EEE, Civil, Mechanical, AI/ML) in a single drive.

Trainable Talent

Graduates possess strong fundamentals, high adaptability, and eagerness to learn proprietary technology stacks quickly.

Fast Recruitment

Streamlined drive coordination by the T&P Cell enables quick testing, interviews, and offer rollouts within tight timelines.

Tier-2 Strategy

Expanding talent acquisition into high-performing regional state institutes reduces attrition rates and hiring competition.

High ROI

Combining competitive salary benchmarks with high productivity yields long-term value for enterprise software and core engineering firms.

🎓 Why Students Choose NCE Chandi

Value drivers for aspiring engineers across Bihar and India

Affordable Education

Extremely nominal tuition fees allow quality engineering education without heavy financial burden or student loans.

Government Recognition

A trusted public institution managed by the Govt. of Bihar and affiliated with Bihar Engineering University (BEU).

Focused Learning Campus

A peaceful, green campus in Chandi ideal for concentrated studies, gate preparation, and competitive programming.

Regular Drives

Consistent campus drives and pool drives from major IT and core companies like TCS, Infosys, and Wipro.

Self-Learning Bandwidth

Curriculum structure leaves room for students to build real-world projects in AI/ML, Full-Stack Development, and Cloud.

High Career Growth

Strong alumni network and meritocratic ecosystem enable dedicated students to secure high-paying off-campus & on-campus offers.

⚖ Strategic Summary

For Companies

NCE Chandi serves as a high-yield talent destination offering cost-effective hiring, strong technical capability, and long-term candidate retention.

For Students

NCE provides a solid, government-backed foundation and affordable degree. Career breakthroughs depend on proactive skill development, practical projects, and continuous dedication.
