About Nalanda College of Engineering, Chandi

A leading government technical institution established by the Department of Science, Technology and Technical Education, Government of Bihar.

Inauguration & Legacy

Founded on 19th November 2008 by Honorable Chief Minister Sri Nitish Kumar, restoring the historic academic legacy of ancient Nalanda through modern technical learning.

Affiliation & Approval

Approved by AICTE (New Delhi) and affiliated with Bihar Engineering University (BEU), Patna, ensuring industry-aligned technical curricula.

Key Academic Branches

  • Computer Science & Engineering
  • Civil Engineering
  • Electrical & Electronics Engineering
  • Mechanical Engineering
  • Aeronautical Engineering

Campus & Innovations

Spanning a modern campus in Chandi equipped with high-tech laboratories, state-of-the-art incubation centres, central library, and active GATE preparation cells.
